1Project Switcher Menu Audit

ProjectSwitcher.tsx · EditorPage.tsx · Menu.tsx
Current Project Switcher Menu
Three separate back-navigation items (Back to Assignments, All projects, Home) bloat the menu; monochrome TeX icons lack format distinction; opaque white box contrasts harshly over the warm Liquid Glass sidebar.

Planned Menu Improvements

1. Remove All projects: Drop All projects from the switcher menu (and keep Back to Assignments + Home as clean folder navigation), eliminating accidental clicks.

2. Spectrum Format Marks: Tint document-kind marks subtly in Spectrum mode — TeX in Writing Orange (#c45001), Typst in Teal (#239dad), Markdown in Flow Blue (#0073df) — so mixed-engine folders read at a glance.

3. macOS 27 Liquid Glass Menu: Apply blur(28px) saturate(1.8) + specular 0.5px top rim + uppercase 11px section heading (ASSIGNMENTS) and concentric 14px → 8px radii.

2Research Sidebar Panel Overhaul

ResearchPanel.tsx · tools.css
Current Research Panel (Reported Screenshot)
8 blue source pills wrap across 3 ragged rows eating 110px of vertical height; year inputs, checkboxes, search buttons, a bordered card, and 2 flat pills compete for hierarchy.

Planned Research Panel Improvements

1. Fold 8 Source Pills into a Popover Chip: Replace the 3-row wall of blue pills with two compact chips right below the search input: Sources · 8 ▾ (opens a Liquid Glass toggle checklist) and Filters ▾ (year range, Preprints, AI sort).

2. Clear Primary vs. Agent Action Row: Pair Search (solid accent pill) with Deep research (Spectrum-tinted sparkle pill) on one balanced 2-column row.

3. Unified Inset Quick-Actions Card: Combine Find citations for selection, Draft related work, and Verify claims into ONE macOS 27 grouped inset card with Spectrum-tinted SF line icons and trailing chevrons.

3To-dos Sidebar Panel Polish

TodosList.tsx · todos.ts · tools.css
Current To-dos Panel (Reported Screenshot)
Clean overall structure, but starter group chips (+ Checking, + Spelling, + References, + Still to write) are flat monochrome outlines disconnected from the Spectrum highlight hues used in the editor.

Planned To-dos Improvements

1. Spectrum-Coded Group Chips: Give each starter group chip its matching Spectrum dot (Checking coral, Spelling amber, References green, Still to write blue) so groups visually tie into the editor’s highlight colours.

2. Elevated Quick-Add Input: Upgrade .todo-add to the unified translucent 32px glass field style (matching the Research box & Settings search).

3. Grouped Starter Card: Place the empty-state prompt and New group starter chips inside a subtle translucent inset card so the sidebar maintains the same visual rhythm as Research and Files.
